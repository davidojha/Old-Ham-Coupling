# ⚙️ Coupling Mechanism

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on the **design, analysis, fabrication, and assembly of a Coupling Mechanism** developed as part of a Mechanical Engineering semester project.

A coupling is a mechanical device used to **connect two shafts and transmit rotational power and torque** from one shaft to another. The project provides practical experience in mechanical design, CAD modelling, machining, fabrication, assembly, and testing.

The main objective was to develop a **simple, reliable, and cost-effective coupling mechanism** capable of transmitting torque while maintaining proper shaft alignment.

## ⚙️ Working Principle

The coupling connects a **driving shaft** to a **driven shaft**.

When the driving shaft rotates, torque is transmitted through the coupling to the driven shaft. The coupling and key arrangement ensure that both shafts rotate together.

Proper alignment of the shafts is important to reduce:

- Vibration
- Wear
- Power loss
- Noise
- Mechanical stress

### Power Transmission

The basic relationship between torque, power, and rotational speed is:

\[
P = \frac{2\pi NT}{60}
\]

Where:

- `P` = Power in watts
- `N` = Rotational speed in RPM
- `T` = Torque in N·m
